# ZAYATHON 2026 - Judge Portal Manual

## Overview
The Judge Portal allows hackathon jury members to inspect assigned team submissions, evaluate slide decks, and enter rubric scores.

## Accessing Judge Portal
1. Log in with your assigned judge credentials (`role = 'judge'`).
2. Click the **Judge** button on the navbar.

## Evaluation Criteria (100 Marks Total)
- **Innovation & Novelty** (25 Marks): Uniqueness of concept and creative problem-solving.
- **Technical Implementation** (25 Marks): Code quality, architecture robustness, and working prototype.
- **Feasibility & Architecture** (20 Marks): Technical viability and system integration.
- **Presentation & Pitch** (15 Marks): Clarity of slide deck and pitch delivery.
- **Real-World Impact** (15 Marks): Target audience scalability and domain relevance.

## Submitting Scores
1. Select an assigned team from the left roster.
2. Review proposal PDF and presentation slides.
3. Adjust sliders for all 5 criteria.
4. Add constructive judge feedback in comments.
5. Click **Submit Final Score**. Leaderboard rankings update instantly!
