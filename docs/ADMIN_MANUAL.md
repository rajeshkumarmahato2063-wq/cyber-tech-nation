# ZAYATHON 2026 - Organizer & Admin Command Center Manual

## Accessing Admin Panel
1. Sign in with an account having `role = 'admin'` or `role = 'organizer'`.
2. Click the **Admin** button in the top sticky navbar.

## Key Admin Workflows

### 1. Registration Approval Workflow
- Navigate to **Registrations**.
- Filter by status (`Pending`, `Under Review`, `Approved`, `Rejected`).
- Click **Inspect** to review proposal PDFs, pitch decks, and team members.
- Approve or Reject entries with custom status notes. Approving automatically generates the team's QR Pass & PDF Certificate.

### 2. QR Attendance Check-In Desk
- Navigate to **QR Check-In**.
- Search for team name or scan participant QR code.
- Click **Check In** to log onsite attendance.

### 3. Broadcasting Announcements
- Navigate to **Announcements**.
- Post notices with category (`General`, `Schedule`, `Rules`, `Urgent`).
- Toggle **Pin to Top** for high-priority alerts.

### 4. Assigning Judges
- Navigate to **Overview** or **Judge Assigner**.
- Pair judges with assigned project submissions.

### 5. CSV & Data Export
- Click **Export CSV** in any table view to download registration and score reports.
